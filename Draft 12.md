# What's new in Colonio 0.2.2 (Draft 12)

## New name
- The game is now called **Colonio** (Колонио).
- The rare resource from planet centres is now **Starium** (Стариум). It still builds leviathans for 100 and wins Conquest at 100.

## Tutorials
- **Clearer guidance:**
  - The text sits at the bottom of the screen, or at the top when a menu or what you need to press is down there. Close it with ✕; the next text appears by itself.
  - Only what the yellow outline shows can be pressed. You can still move and zoom the map, and leave through **Menu** in the top bar.
  - When a menu covers what the outline wants, it points at that menu's ✕ first. When a list is closed, it points at the button that opens it, such as "Build a station here".
  - A correct press no longer sparkles through menus.
  - To make a fleet, a hand shows how: press and hold, then drag a box over the squadrons.
  - A squadron order works from the squadron itself or from its tile's menu.
- **When a lesson ends**, a short summary appears in the text box. Then the outline shows the top bar and Menu, back to the main menu.
- **Starter tutorial:**
  - "Build a station here" is its own step.
  - The new worker's route to the planet centre goes over your iron asteroid.
  - Every attack shows the battle preview.
  - No more "FINISH HIM": the enemy core falls with the new animation.
- **New and reworked lessons** in **Settings → Tutorials:**
  - **Bomber:** the battle preview shows your 4 bombers losing. You build 2 more, and 6 bombers beat 4 + 3 upgraded ones thanks to their bonus.
  - **Stomper:** 4 stompers roll onto a planet centre and shake the 5 enemy tiles around it.
  - **Utility drones:** spy, engineer and charger. The engineer lesson shows the frigates' health before and after they're healed.
  - **Stations:** upgrade station, death turret, and repairing destroyed stations.

## Battles
- **Every effect waits its turn:** after the moves, the death turret and stomper strikes, catapult rockets, ballista bolts and battles all play together. Then boomerangs fly home, then a falling core implodes, then the win or loss screen appears.
- **Nothing looks destroyed too early:** drones, turrets and cores stay on their tile until their battle, rocket, bolt or strike has played.
- **Death turret:** a reticle locks on, then a column of light strikes the tile.
- **Stompers:** they land with a bump, cracks run over the planet, and each tile they hit shows its damage.
- **Ballista bolts** travel hop by hop, with a zap on each hit.
- **The red battle ring** pulses twice, then goes away.
- **Battle sounds:** shots each round, a crackle on each bolt hop, a thud for stompers, a thunder crack for the death turret, and an alarm and blast when a core falls. A falling core is heard from anywhere; the rest gets quieter as you zoom out.
- **Battle animation:** fights on tiles you can see play out round by round, with damage numbers. You can turn it off in **Settings → Battle animations**.
  - Each side shows up to 6 units: the next 6 to take damage. When one falls, the next slides in, and "+N" counts the rest.
  - Turrets show as shields, a leviathan as its big dragon icon, and an attacked core sits in the middle with its health ring dropping.
- **Engineer healing:** a green spark circles each healed squadron, and a ring around it fills from its real health before to after.
- **Core destroyed:** a falling core implodes with a shockwave. The win or loss screen waits until it has finished.
- **Battle preview** for fleets and drawn routes too, not just single squadrons.

## Drones
- **Bomber** is the basic war drone: build more, deal more. The **interceptor** is the fast one for raids and quick replies.
- **Frigate:** its description now says enemies target it before your other space drones.
- **Rovers can be upgraded** at the upgrade station, like other drones. Build the station on a section of their planet.
  - **Tank (upgraded):** uses no energy while standing still, except on a planet centre.
  - **Stomper:** deals no damage in battle. Instead, every time it moves, each stomper deals 0.5 to every enemy on the planet tiles around where it lands. Upgraded, it also deals 1 to everything on the tile it steps onto.
  - **Ballista (upgraded):** a bolt charged to 5 keeps jumping after the 5th hit, as hard as the 5th hit, as long as it finds new enemies on connected tiles.

## Menus
- **Settings** has a new **Sound** tab with separate Music, Effects and Battle volumes. **General** has a new switch, **Fleets follow drawn routes**.
- **Drawing a route:** the whole tile counts at any zoom, and the drag can start anywhere on the unit's tile. Tiles you skip in a fast drag fill in by themselves. Dragging back onto an earlier tile cuts the route there, and a tiny drag no longer cancels the move.
- **Cores heal 10 a turn** while no enemy war drones are on them.
- **Elo change** on the end screen is shown in one line.
- **Empire menu:** energy, every resource and starium as cards with their change per turn, workers, and a storage bar.
- **Squadron menu:**
  - The stats next to the name are gone.
  - A squadron in a fleet has an **Open fleet** tab.
  - **Leave fleet** takes it out of its fleet.
  - **Destroy** asks first and shows what you get back. You only get resources on land connected to your core.
  - Hover over a drone (or tap it on a phone) to destroy just that one.
  - Destroy replaces Self-destruct and Disassemble.
- **New fleet menu:** its squadrons as cards, with Open and Leave on each, plus Move fleet and Disband fleet.
- **Squadron lists** no longer repeat the energy row.
- **Choosing where to move:** the drones you're moving pulse yellow.
- **Leviathan:** its card shows the leviathan.
