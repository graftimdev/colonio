# What's new in Jopium 0.2.1 (Draft 11)

## Tutorials
- **New starter tutorial on a hand-made map:**
  - You start with one worker. It moves, claims and mines an iron asteroid.
  - You build a second worker, research and build a Barrack, order two drones, group them into a fleet and fight off an enemy drone.
  - You claim a planet centre, link it to your core through a planet section, and fly a leviathan into the enemy core. There's a surprise for the final blow.
- **Calmer guidance:** a slow, turning yellow outline shows what to press. When a menu is in the way, it points at the menu's ✕ first, and it shows "Build a station here" and the press-and-hold for fleets. Hints only appear when there's something to explain, and a correct press gives a short yellow shine.
- **Settings → Tutorials** lists small lessons:
  - Trade and Ballista;
  - one for each war drone: interceptor, bomber, frigate, siege, catapult, boomerang, tank and stomper.

  Finished lessons get a tick.

## Achievements
- Nine achievements in your **Profile**: Long shot, Best deal, Eldritch horror, Found you, So nice, Still standing, Mastermind, End game and Early bird.
- They're earned in online matches. A chime plays when you unlock one, and a summary pops up back in the main menu.

## Online matches
- If everyone resigns on the same turn, nobody wins and everyone loses 20 Elo.
- Turn 1000 is a stalemate: the match ends and everyone gets +10 Elo.

## Interface
- **Squadron menus:**
  - Yours show one chip per drone with its health and energy.
  - An enemy's shows a stat card with health, average energy and damage.
  - Tap the unit's name for its card: its picture, stats, description and upgrade.
- **Trade** works again. Give and Get are icon rows, each with its amount.
- **Empire menu:** one Rivals list, at the bottom.
- **"Move to it"** shows your nearest idle worker ("2 hops away"). It only appears on tiles that aren't yours and don't have one of your workers.
- **Game menu (top bar):** closes when you press anywhere else.
- **Settings:**
  - New switch **Show important reports**, off by default. When it's on, the turn report opens by itself after big events such as losing your core.
  - **C** re-centres the map on your core.
  - The + / − / centre buttons are gone; zoom with the wheel or by pinching.
- **Profile:** closes with a ✕, the Save button shows its whole label, and Sign out is red.
- **Sign-in:** a calm rocket animation plays while it loads.
- **Lobby:** the waiting rocket is calmer, and on phones it flies higher so it doesn't cover the text.
- **Map:**
  - Hit tiles give one small pulse.
  - Workers sit just inside the tile's ring.
  - Spies look like other drones, and upgraded drones are filled with their colour.
  - The leviathan's pilot is drawn at worker size.
- **Phones:**
  - Dragging pans the map while you choose where to move. Drag from the unit's own tile to trace a route.
  - If you switch to another app, the music stops, and games against the computer pause.

## Rules
- Chargers dock in barracks and upgrade stations and can be upgraded there.
- Rovers appear in an upgrade station only while docked there, so you can launch them.
- Upkeep shows correctly: 0.5 energy a turn per war drone or rover, 1 for war drones on planets.
- Tech tree: Boreholes and the Rover station swapped places, and the Rover station's link to Barrack turns solid once researched.

## Computer opponents
- They grow their network outward from their own land and reconnect cut-off land, instead of grabbing scattered tiles.
